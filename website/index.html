Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no subscription. If paid extras ever come, everything you have today stays free.

Do I need an AI key?

No. Without a key you search foods and products from a list and scan barcodes. A free Google Gemini key (takes 2 minutes, instructions in the app) unlocks typing or saying whole meals, photos and meal ideas.

Is this a medical app?

No. All values are estimates and the app does not replace medical or dietary advice. It is intended for people aged 18 and over.

Does it work offline?

Yes. You can log without a connection – entries upload automatically once you are back online.

Found a bug or have an idea?

In the app under "More" → "Report a bug & ideas" – or write to hello.trackerapp@gmail.com.
